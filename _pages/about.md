Thank you for visiting my webpage. I use this website to collect my academic information and to keep
a record of my continued learning.

I am graduate of the Untiversity of Tennessee, Knoxville with a PhD in mathematics. My focus during
my doctoral studies was in numerical mathematics, especially in numerical solutions to partial
differential equations. My doctoral research led to the analysis and implementation of an efficient
algorithm to solve the spectral fractional Laplacian.

Prior to that, I earned a BS in applied mathematics and an MS in engineering from the University of
Tennessee at Chattanooga. My MS was focused on computational fluid dynamics with my thesis studying
and implementing a high-order finite volume solver for the Euler equations.

I continue to be interested in numerical solutions to PDEs using high-performance scientifc computing.
and continue to broaden my skills by pursuing topics such as data analysis, advanced statistical
methods, and machine learning. At the same time, I am sharpening my programming skills with Python and
C++.
