Work experience
======

  * University of Tennessee, Knoxville: Mathematics Department

* August 2023 - July 2024: Graduate Research Assistant
  * University of Tennessee, Knoxville: Mathematics Department
  * My final year of doctoral research was funded by my advisor through an NSF grant.

  * University of Tennessee, Knoxville: Mathematics Department

  * University of Tennessee, Knoxville: Mathematics Department
